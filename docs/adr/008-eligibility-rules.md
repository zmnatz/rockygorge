# Eligibility is evaluated from the 2025-26 Senior Club Regulations

The eligibility tool encodes USA Rugby's player-eligibility rules for a two-division club (Rocky Gorge MD1 upper, MD3 lower) from the current Senior Club Regulations. D1 eligibility requires a registered, active player with a combined two NCS Qualifying Matches; D3 eligibility requires active registration plus three lower-division checks — two matches at the lower division, at most three upper-division matches, and lower-division participation at least equal to the upper-division count.

The regulation's two-division clause is written "50% or more of matches played for the upper division team (lower division participation must be greater or equal to the number of upper division matches played)", which is internally ambiguous; the Figure 1 eligibility matrix settles it as `D3 >= D1` (1 D1 + 1 D3 is "Div 1 Only", 2/2 is cross-eligible), so we evaluate `md3 >= md1` and a fourth upper-division match forfeits D3 eligibility (Reg 3.5(d)(i)).

Two explicit exclusions, so they are not "fixed" later: Match Participation counts `Played` + `Substitute` only (Reserve and per-round attendance codes do not count, per the union-admin guidance), and the April 1 registration deadline is deliberately not enforced — `Player Last Registered` is displayed, not tested, because the report carries no season year.
