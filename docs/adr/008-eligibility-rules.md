# Eligibility is evaluated from the 2025-26 Senior Club Regulations

The eligibility tool encodes USA Rugby's player-eligibility rules for a two-division club (Rocky Gorge MD1 upper, MD3 lower) from the current Senior Club Regulations. D1 eligibility requires a combined two NCS Qualifying Matches at any level; D3 eligibility requires three checks — two matches at the lower division, at most three upper-division matches (a fourth reaches the four-game cap and forfeits D3 eligibility), and lower-division participation at least equal to the upper-division count.

The regulation's two-division clause is written "50% or more of matches played for the upper division team (lower division participation must be greater or equal to the number of upper division matches played)", which is internally ambiguous; the Figure 1 eligibility matrix settles it as `D3 >= D1` (1 D1 + 1 D3 is "Div 1 Only", 2/2 is cross-eligible), so we evaluate `md3 >= md1` and a fourth upper-division match forfeits D3 eligibility (Reg 3.5(d)(i)).

Deliberate exclusions, so they are not "fixed" later: Match Participation counts `Played` + `Substitute` only (Reserve and per-round attendance codes do not count, per the union-admin guidance); and the April 1 registration deadline is not enforced — `Player Last Registered` is displayed, not tested, because the report carries no season year.

Rows are joined per player per team by USA ID: because the report emits one row per player per team per competition, same-team rows across competitions are combined into a single participation total (so a player's matches are never lost to a first-row-wins join), and rows with no USA ID are excluded because they cannot be joined to a roster.

The two-match minimums are a deadline, not a current count: per club guidance, a player must be able to reach two matches by Super Regionals. So fixtures still to play count towards those minimums, on either side for the D1 combined minimum and on the lower side only for the D3 minimum, and a player relying on them is shown as "on track" rather than simply eligible. Remaining fixtures are not checked against a player's availability, and they do not affect the four-game cap or the `md3 >= md1` check, which use matches played. Future playoff fixtures have no teams assigned, so they count as possible matches for every side in that division, one per date because a side plays once on a date however many fixtures are on it. Reaching those playoffs isn't guaranteed, so "on track" means a player could still qualify, not that they will. Fixtures in Super Regionals and later competitions don't count, and fixtures postponed without a date don't either.
