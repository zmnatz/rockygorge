# Rocky Gorge

Content and commerce site for Rocky Gorge Rugby Club.

## Language

**Store item**:
A purchasable product in the store (e.g. banquet tickets, dues, sponsorships), identified by a `name`.
_Avoid_: Product

**Option**:
A selectable price point on a store item, made of a display `name` and a numeric `value` (the dollar amount).
_Avoid_: Key-value pair, mapping, pair
